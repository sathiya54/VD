Help Center

Create an account, then use "+ New Entry" from the Diary page or the dashboard to write your first entry. Your writing streak starts counting from your first entry.

Set a title, mood, category and tags, and write freely in the editor. Entries can be marked private, favorited, or moved to Trash (recoverable) instead of deleted outright.

Goals track long-running progress with a percentage and milestones. Tasks are simpler to-dos that can optionally link to a goal or a diary entry.

AI features are optional and off by default. Turn them on from Profile → Settings, then use AI actions (summarize, improve writing, suggest tags) from the entry editor.

See our Privacy Policy for what we collect and why. You can permanently delete your account and all its data at any time.
