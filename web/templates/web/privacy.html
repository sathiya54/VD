Privacy Policy

Last updated: {% now "F Y" %}

What we collect

Your account email and username, the diary entries, moods, categories, tags, goals, tasks, reminders, events and memories you create, and basic usage metadata (timestamps, request logs) needed to operate the service securely.

Why we collect it

Solely to provide the diary, goals, tasks, reminders, calendar, memories and analytics features you use. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How it is stored

Data is stored in our database and file storage, accessible only to your authenticated account. We do not currently encrypt diary content at rest beyond standard database and transport (HTTPS) protections — we will not claim end-to-end encryption unless that is actually implemented.

AI processing

AI features are off by default. If you turn them on in Settings, the specific text you submit to an AI action (e.g. "improve writing") is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a response, and is not used to train their models under our agreement with them. No diary content is sent to AI without you explicitly triggering an AI action.

Notifications

Reminder, task and streak notifications are generated from your own data and delivered in-app (and, on mobile, via your device's notification system once configured). We do not share this data with third-party advertisers.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account and its data from Settings at any time. This is irreversible.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app's support contact listed in the About page.
